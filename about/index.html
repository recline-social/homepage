About Recline · Independent project

About Recline: an independent, creator-owned community platform

Real communities deserve a space of their own. Recline is an independently developed, self-funded platform for people who want smaller, more meaningful online communities—without sacrificing core communication tools or clarity about privacy.

Why it exists

More than a chat feed.

Recline is being built for creators and their most committed members. The goal is a private community space where conversations, participation and recognition can belong together.

01 / Community

Spaces that feel personal

Create communities with channels, roles and invite controls. Shape how members gather, rather than organizing everything around a public feed.

02 / Communication

Conversations first

Text, direct messages and voice/video experiences form the core of the open beta. The aim is to make the basics dependable and welcoming.

03 / Recognition

Contribution matters

Sparks and member participation help communities recognize activity. The financial side has additional safeguards and is being reviewed separately.

The people behind the product

Independently built, not venture-backed.

Recline is currently developed as a self-funded, unincorporated project. It is being developed in public beta while its reliability, privacy and sustainable business model are tested with real people. It is not presented as a registered company or an investor-backed startup.

How it can keep running

A straightforward business model.

Core communication stays free

Recline's beta aims to keep everyday chat, voice and video accessible. The product is not built around targeted ads or selling user content.

Optional contributions and features

Sparks, visual enhancements and the advertised Founding Supporter programme are paths being explored to support infrastructure and development. These are not claims of existing revenue or guaranteed payouts.

A transparent beta

Promises should match the software.

Message-text encryption and other privacy protections are important to Recline, but the service also retains necessary metadata, attachments sit outside current text E2EE protections, and the project has not completed an independent security audit. Read the documented limitations before relying on it for sensitive conversations.
