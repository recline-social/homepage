Public roadmap · No promised ship dates

Building depth before breadth.

Recline is an evolving beta, not a finished communications or financial product. This roadmap separates current capabilities, active hardening work and longer-term possibilities.

Current beta status →About the project
Open beta

Core experience

Features represented in the current product code and publicly described beta. Individual features still need routine end-to-end checks; this isn't a live health monitor.

  • Username-based accounts and Spaces with channels, invites and roles.
  • Text messages, direct messages and real-time presence.
  • Voice/video calling using WebRTC, subject to network and device constraints.
  • Sparks balance, daily rewards and selected non-cash uses.
Being tested

Trust & reliability

Work in progress; not all items are deployed publicly.

  • More expressive profiles: appearance, bio, status, links and privacy settings.
  • Separate development environment, automated checks and reliable backup/recovery practices.
  • Financial controls for Sparks provenance, refunds, payment disputes and creator eligibility.
  • Security testing, better error handling and clearer reporting of service limitations.
Exploration

Future community tools

Ideas, not commitments. Priorities will depend on feedback, safety reviews and ongoing hosting costs.

  • Creator-focused membership and support options.
  • More profile personalization and moderation improvements.
  • Security/privacy improvements, including an external review when feasible.
  • Broader creator monetization only after financial and compliance requirements are satisfied.

Help decide what comes next.

If you're testing Recline, tell us what you tried, what worked, what failed and what would make you return. We prefer reproducible feedback over invented testimonials or vanity metrics.
