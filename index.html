How it works

Your message is locked before it leaves your device.

Channel text is encrypted with a key derived from the community passphrase. DMs use ECDH P-256. The server relays and stores ciphertext.

The problem

Audiences aren't communities.

Existing platforms were built to monetize attention, not to build belonging. Creators don't own their communities. Members have no reason to show up beyond the content itself.

Creators don't own their community
Your community lives on someone else's platform, under their rules, generating their revenue. Discord monetizes your members' attention. Patreon takes a cut of subscriptions. You get neither the data nor the relationship — just access, revocable at any time.
Lurking is the default
In large communities, people can easily become spectators. Recline is exploring daily rewards and member recognition as ways to help participation feel worthwhile.
Private conversations aren't private
A private-channel label alone does not establish end-to-end encryption. Recline focuses on client-side protection of message text while openly documenting metadata, upload and device-side limitations.

The platform

A real community stack.
For real communities.

Community isn't just a chat room. Recline is built for the full loop — private spaces, member contribution, creator support, and recognition that means something.

Creator-owned spaces
You create the community and set its rules. Custom roles, granular permissions, invite controls, moderation tools, and community structure give creators practical control over how their space operates.
roles · permissions · invite links
✦
Sparks economy
Members earn Sparks automatically every day they log in. Spend them to tip creators, send animated messages, or broadcast announcements. Status is earned by showing up — and creator-support and potential earnings features are being evaluated during the beta.
daily rewards · member tips · beta economy
Encrypted channels
Messages are encrypted in your browser before they leave your device. The server stores only ciphertext — architecturally unable to read channel conversations. No policy, no moderator, no database breach changes this.
AES-GCM-256 · PBKDF2-SHA256
Encrypted direct messages
DM text uses ECDH P-256 key agreement with HKDF-SHA256 and AES-GCM. Private key material stays client-controlled; the service stores encrypted message payloads plus delivery metadata.
ECDH P-256 · HKDF-SHA256
Voice & video calls
Recline uses WebRTC with encrypted media transport for voice and video. Connectivity may require relay servers depending on the network. Screen sharing, deafening and per-peer volume are among the beta calling tools.
WebRTC · DTLS-SRTP · P2P
No email or phone required
A standard chat account needs a username and password, not an email or phone number. Payment processors may require separate verification if you opt into eligible payment or payout services.
anonymous signup · Argon2id passwords

Spark economy

Contribution made visible.

Sparks are the community economy — built into the platform, not bolted on. Members earn them by showing up daily. Creators earn them when their community engages. The result is a community where active participation actually means something.

  • Daily login rewards Sparks automatically — no grinding required
  • Tip creators and members directly — 70% goes to the creator
  • Send animated messages that stand out in busy channels
  • Broadcast server-wide announcements to every member
  • Creator withdrawals, where offered, depend on verified earnings, eligibility and provider checks
  • Core chat, voice, and video are offered without payment in the beta

Trust layer

Privacy makes the community real.

Privacy is more than a toggle. Recline aims to encrypt message text on the client, while acknowledging important limitations involving metadata, attachments, account devices and the lack of an independent security audit.

Server can see
Your username (stored in plain text)
Message timestamps
Which channels you belong to
That a message was sent (not content)
Your IP (not logged by default)
Server cannot see
Message content — encrypted before sending
DM text content — encrypted client-side
Your channel passphrase
Email or phone not required for chat signup
Call audio or video — WebRTC is P2P

Open Beta is live. Metadata minimization and cryptographic hardening remain ongoing. Full privacy details, honest limitations, and our data commitments →
